Audience: a developer deciding whether to point an agent at this, and a reader
deciding in fifteen seconds whether the author is serious. Job: understand what

no users. Nothing may be fabricated.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dense text surface at the intersection of lesswrong, hacker news and
reddit: an item list plus an attention signal, no marketing chrome. It refuses
the AI eval hero with a terminal mock, and it refuses a themed costume. There is
no metaphor here and no style goal.

OWN-WORLD: Light neutral paper, near black ink, one palm sap amber for links and
the bar, muted frond green for disposition tags. Serif for argument, mono for
machine text, and that split is the only ornament. Hairline rules, tight
leading, tabular numerals. No cards, no shadows, no gradients, no icon tiles.

STORY: The reader learns what arenga is in one screen, reads the six probes with
what each refuses to measure, sees the attention panel standing empty and marked
coming soon, and leaves with the curl line.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A one line dense bar. The lede at narrow measure, plain. The six
probe rows begin above the fold as full width ruled rows, rank, slug, disposition,
measures against refuses. The curl block is the primary action.

FORM: Brief pinned by the user, which overrides seed 4a1524d9 assignment 5.
Candidate list and roll recorded, not built.
